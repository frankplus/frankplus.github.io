bring-up

Teaching phones to boot something new.

Commercial phones ship with Android vendor drivers and nothing else. On the Volla Phone Plinius, OpenHarmony boots first on the phone's own Android 14 kernel and owns the device. Under OpenHarmony's standard hardware interface, one small plug-in per interface reaches the hardware by one of three routes: Android HALs loaded in-process through libhybris, vendor daemons in a Halium container, or code written straight against the kernel driver.

Volla Phone Plinius · MediaTek Dimensity 7300 · OpenHarmony 7.0 13 working · 3 in progress

  2. Halium containerAndroid vendor services only. No interface, no apps.ok
  3. Display1080×2400 OLED. Graphics shared with the HAL, never copied.ok
  4. GPUMali-G615 at full frame rateok

  9. Wi-FiJoins networks with real traffic, straight on the kernel driverok
  10. SensorsSensors, vibration and brightness, straight on the kernel driversok
  11. AppsApp Store, browser, media and gamesok
  12. Android appsOptional Waydroid container. Each app opens as an ordinary OpenHarmony window.ok
  13. LocationGPS positioning through OpenHarmony's location serviceok
